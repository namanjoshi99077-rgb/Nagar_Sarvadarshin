# 🏙️ Nagar Sarvadarshin

### AI-Powered Mobile Civic Intelligence & Issue Resolution Platform

> **See the Problem. Locate the Impact. Resolve the City.**

Nagar Sarvadarshin is a **mobile-first AI-powered civic intelligence platform** that enables citizens to report real-world urban problems using their **mobile camera, live GPS location, voice and multimedia evidence**.

The platform uses AI-assisted classification, duplicate detection, priority scoring, smart department routing, GIS visualization and resolution verification to transform fragmented citizen reports into unified **Civic Cases**.

> **ONE PROBLEM. ONE CIVIC CASE. ONE COORDINATED RESPONSE.**

---

## 🚀 Problem

Cities receive thousands of complaints about:

- 🕳️ Potholes and road damage
- 🚧 Road obstructions
- 🚗 Illegal parking
- 🚦 Traffic signal issues
- 🚌 Public transport infrastructure
- 🚶 Pedestrian safety hazards
- 🗑️ Waste management
- 💧 Water leakage
- 💡 Street infrastructure
- ⚠️ Public safety issues

The same physical problem can be reported by many citizens, creating duplicate complaints and inefficient municipal workflows.

Traditional complaint systems often lack:

- Real-time location
- Visual evidence
- Intelligent prioritization
- Duplicate detection
- Geographic intelligence
- Automated department routing
- Resolution verification
